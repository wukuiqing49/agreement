Account Deletion Agreement 账号注销协议

Effective Date: September 15, 2026 App Version: V1.0.2

1. Overview 1. 协议概述

This Account Deletion Agreement sets forth the terms under which you may terminate your user profile. Initiating the deletion of your account represents a permanent termination of the service agreement between you and SiteReport. Please read these terms carefully before proceeding.

本账号注销协议规定了您终止并抹除 SiteReport 用户账号的条款与规则。注销账号代表着您主动终止与 SiteReport 之间享有的账号使用合同关系。这是一项不可逆转的操作，请您在开始前仔细阅读本协议所有条款。

WARNING: Deleting local app data is permanent and cannot be undone. Google Play subscriptions and purchase history are controlled by Google and are not cancelled or deleted by this action.

警告：删除应用本地数据后无法恢复。Google Play 订阅和购买历史由 Google 管理，本操作不会自动取消订阅或删除 Google Play 交易记录。

2. Conditions for Deletion 2. 注销应当满足的前提条件

Before deletion, make sure you understand and complete the following:

注销前请确认并完成以下事项：

  • Sign in to the profile you want to remove, or use the support email below if you cannot access the app.
  • 登录需要清理的账号；如无法进入应用，可使用下方支持邮箱提交请求。
  • Export any inspection reports, photos, templates, or server settings you need to keep.
  • 提前导出需要保留的巡检报告、照片、模板或服务器配置。

3. Data Impact & Loss of Entitlements 3. 数据与权限的后续处理与影响

Upon successful completion of the account deletion process, the following consequences will immediately occur:

一旦您的注销申请处理完成，将对您的资产及数据产生如下即时影响：

  1. Identity Disconnection: The local Google session is removed and the active DevHub installation-to-Google identity association is cleared. This does not delete your Google Account or Google Play records. 身份关联解除： 应用会清除本地 Google 登录会话，并解除当前 DevHub 安装与 Google 身份的关联；该操作不会删除您的 Google 账号或 Google Play 记录。
  2. Google Play Entitlement: Deletion does not cancel a subscription or erase a purchase. A valid entitlement may be restored later through the same Google Play account, subject to Google's billing state. Google Play 权益： 注销不会取消订阅或抹除购买记录。后续使用同一 Google Play 账号时，有效权益仍可依据 Google 的账单状态恢复。
  3. Local Device Storage: The in-app deletion flow clears SiteReport-owned local databases, inspection photos, templates, signatures, server profiles, retry queues, and private report files. Files previously exported to shared storage or a user-managed server remain under your control and must be deleted separately. 本地设备数据： 应用内注销流程会清除 SiteReport 管理的本地数据库、巡检照片、模板、签名、服务器配置、重试队列及私有报告文件。此前已导出到共享存储或用户自管服务器的文件仍由您控制，需要另行删除。

4. Deletion Steps 4. 具体的注销路径与操作流程

You can initiate and complete the account deletion directly through the app without manual audit delays:

您可以在 SiteReport App 内部自助发起并完成账号注销，无需人工后台审核，操作步骤如下：

  1. Open the App and tap the "Settings" (设置) tab on the main interface.
  2. 打开 SiteReport 应用程序，进入底栏的“Settings” (设置) 页面。
  3. Scroll down to locate and tap "Account Deletion" (账号注销) or "Delete Account" (删除账号).
  4. 向下滑动找到并点击“Account Deletion” (账号注销) 选项。
  5. Read the on-screen data-loss warning and confirm the deletion.
  6. 阅读屏幕上的数据丢失提示并确认注销。
  7. The app signs out, clears SiteReport-owned local data, and requests DevHub identity disconnection.
  8. 应用将退出登录、清除 SiteReport 管理的本地数据，并请求解除 DevHub 身份关联。

If you encounter any issues during the process or cannot access your account, you can also send a deletion request to our support email wukuiqing@gmail.com. We will process your verified request within 7 business days.

如果您在注销过程中遇到界面无法点击，或者您的 Google 登录组件失效，您也可以向我们的官方技术邮箱 wukuiqing@gmail.com 投递注销申请邮件。我们在核实您账户所有权后，将在 7 个工作日内帮您手动完成注销。

5. Legal Disclaimer 5. 法律声明与补充条款

DevHub purchase-verification records are not erased by the in-app identity-disconnection call. You may request deletion through the support email above. Records required for entitlement restoration, security, fraud prevention, disputes, or legal obligations may be retained for the necessary period and restricted or de-identified where appropriate. Google Play records are controlled by Google.

应用内解除身份关联不会自动删除 DevHub 的历史购买校验记录。您可以通过上述支持邮箱申请删除；为恢复权益、安全、防欺诈、争议处理或法定义务所必需的记录，可能在必要期限内保留，并在适用时限制处理或去标识化。Google Play 记录由 Google 管理。
