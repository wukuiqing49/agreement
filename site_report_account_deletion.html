Account Deletion Agreement 账号注销协议

Effective Date: June 24, 2026 App Version: V1.0.1

1. Overview 1. 协议概述

This Account Deletion Agreement sets forth the terms under which you may terminate your user profile. Initiating the deletion of your account represents a permanent termination of the service agreement between you and SiteReport. Please read these terms carefully before proceeding.

本账号注销协议规定了您终止并抹除 SiteReport 用户账号的条款与规则。注销账号代表着您主动终止与 SiteReport 之间享有的账号使用合同关系。这是一项不可逆转的操作，请您在开始前仔细阅读本协议所有条款。

WARNING: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All Google Cloud synchronized profile metadata, app subscription bindings, and Pro features linked to this account will be permanently deleted and cannot be restored.

警告：账号注销属于不可恢复的永久操作。注销后，您云端所关联的 Google 登录档案元数据、付费专业版绑定记录以及所有的云端传输配置都将被彻底清空且无法找回。

2. Conditions for Deletion 2. 注销应当满足的前提条件

To successfully execute account deletion, your account must meet the following criteria:

为了保障您的权益并确保账号安全，在您提交注销申请时，您的账号需同时满足以下条件：

  • The account must be logged in and verified via Google Sign-In or credentials to prove true ownership.
  • 账号处于正常登录状态，且已通过 Google Sign-In 进行身份校验，证明是您本人拥有的账号。
  • There are no pending commercial disputes or active chargeback negotiations related to in-app purchases.
  • 该账号不存在任何未完结的商业纠纷，且没有正在进行中的 Google Play 账单退款争议或恶意拒付记录。

3. Data Impact & Loss of Entitlements 3. 数据与权限的后续处理与影响

Upon successful completion of the account deletion process, the following consequences will immediately occur:

一旦您的注销申请处理完成，将对您的资产及数据产生如下即时影响：

  1. Cloud Profile Erasure: All user profile information, Google credentials, and metadata synchronized to the Firebase cloud environment will be permanently removed. 云端资产清空： 所有同步至 Firebase 或 Google Cloud 的基本账户档案、绑定信息及同步元数据将被直接彻底抹除。
  2. Pro License Termination: Any purchased Pro licenses (unlimited items, watermark-free exports, batch exporting privileges) associated with this account will expire immediately. We do not provide refunds or credit transfers for unused subscription periods. 付费专业版失效： 由于您的订阅和购买记录是与账号绑定的，注销代表您主动放弃已购买的专业版（免水印、无限制项目、批量导出等）特权，剩余的订阅天数无法折现、退款，亦无法转移至新账号。
  3. Local Device Storage: Since local inspection databases, photos, and generated PDF reports reside strictly inside your local device sandbox, deleting the account does not automatically clear these local files. To wipe them, you must uninstall the app or clear App Data through Android system settings. 本地沙盒存储说明： 因为巡检数据库、现场拍照都保存在您设备本地的私有存储中，注销账号本身不会自动抹除您手机里已有的本地草稿。如果您希望一并清除本地缓存，需手动卸载应用或在手机的“设置”中执行“清除应用数据”。

4. Deletion Steps 4. 具体的注销路径与操作流程

You can initiate and complete the account deletion directly through the app without manual audit delays:

您可以在 SiteReport App 内部自助发起并完成账号注销，无需人工后台审核，操作步骤如下：

  1. Open the App and tap the "Settings" (设置) tab on the main interface.
  2. 打开 SiteReport 应用程序，进入底栏的“Settings” (设置) 页面。
  3. Scroll down to locate and tap "Account Deletion" (账号注销) or "Delete Account" (删除账号).
  4. 向下滑动找到并点击“Account Deletion” (账号注销) 选项。
  5. Read the on-screen warnings, confirm, and verify your identity again via Google re-authentication.
  6. 阅读屏幕上弹出的风险提示，点击确认，并按照提示重新调用 Google 进行一次安全身份验证。
  7. Click "Confirm Delete". The system will execute the deletion script, log you out, and return to the login interface.
  8. 点击“确认注销”，系统将立即执行删除脚本，清除您的在线状态并退出登录返回首屏。

If you encounter any issues during the process or cannot access your account, you can also send a deletion request to our support email wukuiqing@gmail.com. We will process your verified request within 7 business days.

如果您在注销过程中遇到界面无法点击，或者您的 Google 登录组件失效，您也可以向我们的官方技术邮箱 wukuiqing@gmail.com 投递注销申请邮件。我们在核实您账户所有权后，将在 7 个工作日内帮您手动完成注销。

SiteReport reserves the right to retain transactional logs or financial invoicing history to comply with relevant local tax regulations, national financial monitoring rules, or anti-money laundering legislation, even after your account metadata is erased.

在您注销账号后，对于您此前发生的应用内购买历史，SiteReport 将根据国家财税、金融监管及反洗钱法律法规的硬性规定，在法定最短时限内仅保留必要的订单对账及财务存根流水，该等数据将与您的个人身份档案完全脱钩并进行匿名化隔离。
