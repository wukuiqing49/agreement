隐私政策

生效及最后更新日期：2026 年 7 月 31 日

Batch Photo 是一款以设备本地处理为主的 Android 图片工具。本政策说明图片处理、Google 服务、附近传输及第三方 SDK 涉及的信息处理活动。

1. 适用范围与原则

本政策适用于包名为 com.wkq.photo 的 Batch Photo Android 应用。运营者为 Batch Photo 开发者。我们遵循最小必要、目的明确和本地优先原则。除本政策列明的登录、购买、可选广告/统计、模型资源交付、应用内评价及附近设备传输外，普通图片编辑不会将您的图片上传至开发者服务器。

当前版本没有由开发者运营的云相册、图片云处理或云端账号数据库。Google 登录资料保存在应用私有存储中，用于显示登录状态和关联购买权益。

2. 应用功能

  • 批量压缩、缩放、裁剪、旋转、翻转、格式转换、拼图、水印、标注、模糊和马赛克。
  • EXIF、IPTC、XMP 等元数据读取、编辑与隐私清理。
  • RAW 显影、PDF 导出、二维码/条码生成与识别、图片加密与解密。
  • 本地人像分割、背景替换、人脸检测和证件照排版。
  • 通过附近设备连接在用户确认的设备之间传输文件。
  • Google 登录、Google Play 购买/订阅、恢复购买、应用内评价，以及发布版本按配置启用的 Firebase Analytics 或 Google Mobile Ads。

6. 本地存储、备份与保留

  • 登录会话：保留至退出登录、清除应用数据或卸载；会话文件已排除在 Android 云备份和设备迁移之外。
  • 任务记录：本地最多保留 24 条摘要，且已排除在 Android 云备份和设备迁移之外。
  • 设置与权益缓存：保留至修改、清除应用数据或卸载；部分非敏感设置可能按 Android 备份机制迁移。
  • 临时文件：通常在操作结束、手动清理缓存或后续启动清理；受管理的旧缓存通常在约 24 小时后清理，异常退出可能使其保留更久。
  • 导出和接收文件：保留在相册、下载、自选目录或应用私有接收目录，直至手动删除、清除应用数据（仅私有目录）或卸载。
  • 第三方记录：购买、统计和广告信息由相应服务商依其政策与法律义务保留。

7. 共享、跨境与安全

我们不会出售您的图片或个人信息。仅在完成您请求的功能、取得授权、履行法律义务或保护安全所必需时，向本政策列明的处理者提供必要信息。Google 等服务商可能在您所在地区之外处理数据，并依其传输机制提供保护。

应用使用 Android 私有存储、系统选择器、临时 URI 授权、HTTPS 和受控缓存目录。附近传输包含设备认证确认。任何系统均无法保证绝对安全，请在分享或传输前确认目标和文件内容。

8. 您的选择与权利

  • 不登录也可使用不依赖账号或付费权益的本地功能。
  • 可通过系统选择器控制文件访问，并随时撤回相机、附近设备、通知等权限。
  • 可退出登录，并按《账号注销协议与数据删除说明》清除数据、撤销 Google 授权和管理订阅。
  • 可按适用法律请求访问、更正、删除、限制或反对我们实际持有的个人信息。

9. 儿童隐私

Batch Photo 不专门面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会明知而收集其个人信息。适用地区规定更高数字同意年龄的，以当地法律为准。监护人认为儿童提供了信息时可联系我们。

Batch Photo Legal Document

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: July 31, 2026

Batch Photo is an Android image utility designed primarily for on-device processing. This policy describes information processing related to image tools, Google services, nearby transfer, and third-party SDKs.

1. Scope and Principles

This policy applies to the Batch Photo Android app with package name com.wkq.photo. The operator is the Batch Photo developer. We follow data minimization, purpose limitation, and local-first processing. Except for sign-in, purchases, optional ads/analytics, model delivery, in-app review, and nearby transfer described here, ordinary image editing does not upload images to a developer-operated server.

The current version has no developer-operated cloud gallery, cloud image processing, or cloud account database. Google profile details are stored in app-private storage to show sign-in state and associate purchase entitlements.

2. App Features

  • Batch compression, resizing, cropping, rotation, flipping, conversion, collage, watermarking, markup, blur, and redaction.
  • Reading, editing, and removing EXIF, IPTC, XMP, and other metadata.
  • RAW development, PDF export, QR/barcode creation and recognition, and image encryption/decryption.
  • On-device portrait segmentation, background replacement, face detection, and ID-photo layout.
  • File transfer between user-confirmed devices through nearby connections.
  • Google Sign-In, Google Play purchases/subscriptions, purchase restoration, in-app review, and optionally configured Firebase Analytics or Google Mobile Ads.

6. Local Storage, Backup, and Retention

  • Sign-in session: retained until sign-out, app-data clearing, or uninstall; excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.
  • Task history: up to 24 local summaries; excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.
  • Settings and entitlement cache: retained until changed, app data is cleared, or uninstall; some non-sensitive settings may migrate through Android backup.
  • Temporary files: generally removed after an operation, manual cache cleanup, or a later startup cleanup; managed stale cache is generally cleaned after about 24 hours, but abnormal exits may extend retention.
  • Exported and received files: remain in Photos, Downloads, selected folders, or app-private received storage until manual deletion, app-data clearing (private storage only), or uninstall.
  • Third-party records: purchase, analytics, and ad data is retained under provider policies and legal duties.

7. Sharing, International Processing, and Security

We do not sell images or personal information. Necessary data is provided to listed processors only to complete requested features, with authorization, to comply with law, or to protect safety. Providers such as Google may process data outside your region under their transfer safeguards.

The app uses Android private storage, system pickers, temporary URI grants, HTTPS, and controlled cache directories. Nearby transfer uses device-authentication confirmation. No system is absolutely secure; verify destinations and file contents before sharing or transfer.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Local features that do not need an account or paid entitlement can be used without sign-in.
  • You control file access through system pickers and may revoke camera, nearby, notification, and other permissions.
  • You may sign out and follow the Account Deletion Agreement & Data Deletion Notice to erase data, revoke Google access, and manage subscriptions.
  • Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection for information we actually hold.

9. Children's Privacy

Batch Photo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. A higher digital-consent age required in your region applies. Guardians may contact us if they believe a child provided information.

10. Updates and Contact

We may update this policy when features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The update date will change, and material changes will be communicated in the app or by another reasonable method.
