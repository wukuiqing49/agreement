Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: October 27, 2026

You can delete your Before After Photo Report account in the app or request deletion by email without reinstalling or signing in to the app.

This page is provided by 上海促动科技有限公司, the operator of Before After Photo Report.

Account deletion, local files, and subscriptions are separate. Deleting your app account does not delete your Google account, automatically remove your local photo projects, or cancel a Google Play subscription.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Before After Photo Report.
  2. Go to My → Google Account.
  3. Sign in to the account you want to delete if needed.
  4. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

This action attempts to permanently delete your Firebase Authentication account and clears the app’s current sign-in identity. Firebase may require a recent sign-in. If deletion fails, sign in again and retry, or use the email option below. Signing out alone does not delete an account.

2. Request account and data deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, or want associated service records reviewed for deletion, send a request to wukuiqing@gmail.com. You do not need to install the app to use this option.

Request account & data deletion

Suggested subject: Before After Photo Report — Account and data deletion request

Include the email address used to sign in and identify the data you want removed, such as your app account, associated service records, or feedback. If you used the app without an account, explain which feedback or other service interaction your request concerns.

We may ask for proportionate proof of ownership before deleting account-related data. Do not send your Google password, sign-in code, authentication token, or payment card details. If the button does not open an email app, copy the email address above into your preferred email service.

3. What deletion covers

  • App account: successful in-app deletion removes the Firebase Authentication user for this app. It does not delete your underlying Google account.
  • Associated service data: the current in-app flow clears the active DevHub identity but does not provide an automatic purge of all service records. Use the email request above for deletion of associated account records or feedback.
  • Records that may need to remain: some transaction, security, dispute, or legally required records may need to be retained. When handling your request, we will explain applicable exceptions and any retention information relevant to your case.

After ownership verification and review, we will respond with the outcome or any additional steps needed. This page does not promise instant deletion of all service or provider-held records.

4. Remove photos and project data from your device

Photo projects are stored locally by default. You can delete projects using the app’s project controls. To remove all app-private data from this device, use Android Settings → Apps → Before After Photo Report → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Exact Android menu names may vary.

Export anything you want to keep first. Clearing app storage removes local work. Account deletion does not perform this local cleanup for you. Original photos in the system photo library, exported PDFs or images, and copies sent to others must be removed separately from their respective locations.

5. Cancel subscriptions separately

Deleting your account or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. Manage it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or visit Google Play subscriptions. Cancellation and refund requests are handled under Google Play’s applicable processes.
