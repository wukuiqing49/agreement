RoofQuote Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026 · App: RoofQuote

RoofQuote is an offline roofing measurement, material takeoff, estimating and PDF proposal application for individual roofers and small roofing contractors. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Information you enter

You may enter project names, customer names, property addresses, phone numbers, roof measurements, material prices, contractor profile details, notes and proposal terms. This information is used only to calculate estimates, save projects locally, and generate or share proposal PDFs.

2. Storage and sharing

  • Project and contractor information is stored locally on your device using the app database and local preferences.
  • RoofQuote does not require a RoofQuote cloud account or a RoofQuote server for its core workflow.
  • When you export a backup or PDF, the file is created on your device. When you choose to share it, Android sends it to the app or service you select. That recipient's privacy policy then applies.
  • Unencrypted network traffic is disabled by the app. Optional third-party services may still communicate with their own services as described below.

3. Google services and purchases

RoofQuote includes Google Play Billing for optional Pro purchases. Google Play may process purchase, payment and entitlement information under Google's policies. RoofQuote does not receive your payment card number.

RoofQuote includes Firebase Analytics and the current application contains a Firebase configuration. Analytics collection is enabled by the app configuration unless the user or a future build disables it. Firebase may process device, app-use and diagnostic information under Google's policies. Google advertising integration is included but disabled in the current application configuration. Review the Google privacy disclosures shown in Google Play for the version you install.

4. Permissions

The app requests network access for Google Play and optional Google services. It does not use satellite imagery or require a server for roof calculations. Files shared outside the app use Android's temporary, permission-scoped content URIs.

5. Retention and deletion

Local project data remains on the device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or replace it through a restore/import action. Exported backups and PDFs are separate files and must be deleted from the location where you saved or shared them. See RoofQuote Data Deletion Policy for steps.

6. Children

RoofQuote is a professional estimating tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated with each revision. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the support contact shown on the RoofQuote Google Play listing and include the app version and device details without sending customer data unless necessary.

8. Your choices

You control the information entered into RoofQuote, whether to export or share files, and whether to use optional purchase or third-party features. You can stop local processing by deleting the relevant projects or clearing the app data.

RoofQuote 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 19 日 · 应用：RoofQuote

RoofQuote 是面向个体 Roofer 和小型 Roofing Contractor 的离线屋顶测量、材料算量、报价及 PDF 报价单工具。本政策说明应用会处理哪些信息以及如何使用这些信息。

1. 您主动输入的信息

您可以输入项目名称、客户姓名、施工地址、电话号码、屋顶尺寸、材料价格、承包商资料、备注和报价条款。这些信息仅用于计算估算、在本地保存项目，以及生成或分享报价 PDF。

2. 存储与分享

  • 项目资料和承包商资料通过本地数据库及本地偏好设置保存在您的设备上。
  • RoofQuote 的核心流程不要求 RoofQuote 云账号，也不依赖 RoofQuote 自有服务器。
  • 导出备份或 PDF 时，文件先在设备本地生成；您主动分享后，Android 会将文件交给您选择的应用或服务，接收方的隐私政策将适用。
  • 应用默认禁止明文网络流量；可选的第三方服务仍可能按其自身规则与其服务器通信。

3. Google 服务与购买

RoofQuote 集成 Google Play Billing，用于可选的 Pro 购买。Google Play 可能依据其政策处理购买、支付和权益信息；RoofQuote 不会获得您的银行卡号。

RoofQuote 集成 Firebase Analytics，当前应用也包含 Firebase 配置。除非用户或后续版本关闭，应用配置默认启用 Analytics 数据收集。Firebase 可能依据 Google 政策处理设备、应用使用和诊断信息。Google 广告集成已包含在项目中，但当前应用配置默认关闭广告。请同时查看 Google Play 中对应版本展示的 Google 隐私说明。

4. 权限

应用使用网络权限支持 Google Play 和可选的 Google 服务。应用不使用卫星影像，也不要求服务器完成屋顶计算。对外分享的文件使用 Android 临时且受权限限制的 content URI。

5. 保存期限与删除

本地项目数据会一直保留，直到您删除项目、清除应用存储、卸载应用，或通过恢复/导入操作替换数据。已导出的备份和 PDF 是独立文件，必须在保存或分享它们的位置单独删除。具体步骤请参阅RoofQuote 数据删除政策。

6. 儿童隐私

RoofQuote 是专业报价工具，不面向儿童。我们不会故意要求儿童提供个人信息。

7. 政策变更与联系

当应用功能或法律要求发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。每次修订都会更新上方生效日期。如有隐私问题或删除请求，请通过 RoofQuote Google Play 页面展示的开发者支持联系方式联系，并尽量只提供应用版本和设备信息，除非确有必要，不要发送客户资料。

8. 您的选择

您可以控制输入 RoofQuote 的信息、是否导出或分享文件，以及是否使用可选的购买或第三方功能。删除相关项目或清除应用数据即可停止本地处理。
