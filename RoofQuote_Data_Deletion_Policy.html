RoofQuote Data Deletion Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026 · App: RoofQuote

RoofQuote is designed around local, offline project storage. This policy explains how to delete information created or stored by the app.

1. Delete individual projects

  1. Open RoofQuote and select the project list.
  2. Use the delete action on the project you want to remove and confirm.
  3. To remove a single roof section, open the project, use the section delete action, and confirm.

2. Delete contractor profile and local app data

Contractor profile fields, material rules and other local preferences are stored on the device. To remove all remaining local RoofQuote data, use Android Settings > Apps > RoofQuote > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall RoofQuote. The exact labels may differ by Android version.

3. Delete exported files

Backup JSON files and proposal PDFs exported or shared by you are outside the app database. Delete them from Downloads, Files, cloud drives, email, messaging apps, or other destinations where you saved or sent them. Ask recipients to delete copies you no longer want them to retain.

4. Google Play purchases

Clearing RoofQuote data does not erase Google Play transaction records or automatically cancel a purchase. Manage purchases, refunds and subscriptions through Google Play. Google handles those records under its own policies.

5. Requests and limitations

Because core RoofQuote project data is stored locally, we normally cannot access or delete it remotely. If you believe information was sent to an optional third-party service, contact that service directly using its account or privacy controls. For help, contact the developer through the support contact shown on the RoofQuote Google Play listing.

6. Before deleting

Deletion may be permanent. Export a backup first only if you need a copy; then delete the backup as described above when it is no longer needed.

RoofQuote 数据删除政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 19 日 · 应用：RoofQuote

RoofQuote 采用本地离线项目存储。本政策说明如何删除应用创建或保存的信息。

1. 删除单个项目

  1. 打开 RoofQuote，进入项目列表。
  2. 在需要删除的项目上使用删除操作并确认。
  3. 如需删除单个屋顶分块，进入项目后使用该分块的删除操作并确认。

2. 删除承包商资料和本地应用数据

承包商资料、材料规则和其他本地偏好保存在设备上。要删除 RoofQuote 剩余的本地数据，请进入 Android“设置”>“应用”>“RoofQuote”>“存储”>“清除存储”，或卸载 RoofQuote。不同 Android 版本的名称可能略有不同。

3. 删除已导出的文件

导出的 JSON 备份和报价 PDF 不属于应用数据库。请在下载目录、文件管理器、云盘、邮箱、即时通讯应用或其他保存/发送位置删除它们；如已发送给他人，请联系接收方删除不再需要的副本。

4. Google Play 购买记录

清除 RoofQuote 数据不会删除 Google Play 的交易记录，也不会自动取消购买。购买、退款和订阅请通过 Google Play 管理，相关记录由 Google 按其政策处理。

5. 请求与限制

由于 RoofQuote 的核心项目数据保存在本地，我们通常无法远程访问或删除这些数据。如果您认为信息已发送给可选的第三方服务，请直接使用该服务的账号或隐私控制功能处理。如需帮助，请通过 RoofQuote Google Play 页面展示的开发者支持联系方式联系。

6. 删除前提示

删除可能无法恢复。如需保留副本，请先导出备份；不再需要时，也应按照上述说明删除该备份。
