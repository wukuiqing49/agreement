Privacy notice

About this notice

NASFileManager (com.wkq.nas) is an Android tool for browsing, downloading, uploading and synchronizing files with storage you choose. Basic NAS use does not require Google sign-in. This notice describes the current app; available services depend on the installed version and configuration.

Files, connections and local records

The app processes files, folder paths and metadata to perform your requested browsing, preview, transfer and sync operations. Connection settings, saved credentials, task history, sync plans, preferences and preview caches are stored on your device. Saved passwords use Android Keystore-backed encryption; connection names, paths and task metadata are not all encrypted. Files are transferred between your device and the NAS or storage provider you select. The app does not route your NAS file contents through a developer-operated file-storage service.

Optional Google sign-in

Google handles authentication. The app processes the returned sign-in credential and stores your display name and email locally to show your profile. It does not persist the Google ID token or create a separate developer-hosted account. Google sign-in is independent of your NAS login and Google Play purchase account. Google processes its service data under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Where purchases are configured and available, Google Play processes payment. The app processes purchase status, purchase tokens and verification information to grant or restore Pro access and caches entitlement information locally. The app does not collect your payment-card details. Signing out or clearing app data does not cancel a purchase or issue a refund; manage purchases through Google Play.

Optional online services and activity statistics

Eligible Play/AAB builds support DevHub online services and core activity statistics only after you enable them. Debug and ordinary Release APK builds disable collection. When enabled, requests may include a random installation identifier, app version, activation time and daily activity; the receiving service also sees your network IP address. The integration disables device-detail and locale collection and does not pass NAS addresses, credentials, file names or file contents to DevHub. You can withdraw consent in Me → Help and about → the privacy/data explanation page. Turning it off stops new requests and clears pending statistics; it does not erase previously received server records. Server-side retention periods have not yet been confirmed for publication.

Permissions, sharing and external services

Folder access is limited by the Android permissions and storage locations you grant. Network access is used for selected servers, local discovery and enabled online services. Notifications can show transfer progress. When you share a file, open it in another app, export a report or send feedback, the selected recipient or app receives the information you choose. Reports can contain file paths; review them before sending. A NAS or third-party service may retain its own access logs.

Retention, removal and security

Local information remains until you remove it using the applicable app controls or Android storage controls. Cache cleanup is subject to Android and app behavior; no fixed automatic deletion deadline is promised. Remove saved connections and eligible history in the app. Android Clear storage removes app-private data, including app-private files, so export anything you need first. NAS files, external downloads, exported reports and recovery copies must be managed separately. Security depends on your device, network and server; prefer secure protocols and valid certificates.

隐私说明

关于本说明

NASFileManager（com.wkq.nas）是一款 Android 工具，用于浏览、下载、上传及同步您选择的存储中的文件。基础 NAS 功能无需 Google 登录。本说明描述当前应用行为；可用服务取决于所安装的版本与配置。

文件、连接与本地记录

应用为执行您请求的浏览、预览、传输与同步而处理文件、文件夹路径及元数据。连接配置、已保存凭据、任务历史、同步计划、偏好和预览缓存保存在设备上。已保存密码使用基于 Android Keystore 的加密；连接名称、路径和任务元数据并非全部加密。文件在设备与您选择的 NAS 或存储服务之间传输，应用不会通过开发者运营的文件存储服务中转 NAS 文件内容。

可选 Google 登录

Google 负责身份验证。应用处理返回的登录凭据，并在本机保存显示名称和邮箱以展示资料，不持久化 Google ID token，也不创建独立的开发者服务器账户。Google 登录独立于 NAS 登录和 Google Play 购买账户。Google 按其自身隐私政策处理其服务数据。

购买

在已配置并开放购买的版本中，Google Play 处理付款。应用处理购买状态、购买令牌及验证信息以授予或恢复 Pro 权益，并在本地缓存权益信息。应用不收集银行卡资料。退出登录或清除应用数据不会取消购买或发起退款；请通过 Google Play 管理购买。

可选在线服务与活跃统计

符合条件的 Play/AAB 版本仅在您开启后使用 DevHub 在线服务和基础活跃统计。Debug 与普通 Release APK 禁用采集。启用后，请求可能包含随机安装标识、应用版本、激活时间及每日活跃信息；接收服务也会获知网络 IP 地址。当前集成关闭设备详情与语言采集，不向 DevHub 传递 NAS 地址、凭据、文件名或文件内容。您可以在“我的 → 帮助与关于 → 隐私/数据说明”中撤回同意。关闭后停止新请求并清除待发送统计，但不会删除服务器已接收的记录。服务器端保留期限尚待确认后发布。

权限、分享与外部服务

文件夹访问受您授予的 Android 权限和存储位置限制。网络用于连接所选服务器、局域网发现及已开启的在线服务。通知可以显示传输进度。当您分享文件、使用其他应用打开、导出报告或发送反馈时，所选接收方或应用会获得您选择的信息。报告可能包含文件路径，请在发送前检查。NAS 或第三方服务可能保留自己的访问日志。

保留、删除与安全

本地信息会保留至您通过相应应用功能或 Android 存储设置移除。缓存清理由 Android 和应用行为决定，不承诺固定的自动删除期限。您可在应用内移除已保存连接及可清理的历史记录。Android“清除存储空间”会移除应用私有数据，包括应用私有文件，请先导出需要保留的内容。NAS 文件、外部下载、导出报告及恢复副本须单独管理。安全性也取决于设备、网络和服务器，建议使用安全协议及有效证书。
