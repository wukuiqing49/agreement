Account Cancellation Agreement

Effective Date: November 2, 2026  |  Application: LensDock
Important Notice: LensDock operates on a Local-First architecture. Please review this Agreement before initiating any data reset, account unlinking, or application removal.

1. Standalone & Guest Mode Operation

LensDock does not require centralized user registration for camera tethering, shooting sequence triggers, or photo review. You can perform complete local data erasure at any time via:

  • Selecting "Clear Storage & Cache" in Android System App Settings; or
  • Uninstalling the LensDock app from your Android mobile device.

2. Third-Party Integrations & Cloud Disconnection

If you have configured third-party connections, the following erasure procedures apply:

  • Google Cloud Sync / OAuth Disconnection: Unlinking removes cached OAuth tokens from local storage immediately. You can also revoke access permanently in your Google Account Security Settings. Existing photos already saved to your personal Google Drive are not deleted.
  • Personal Network Attached Storage (NAS): Disconnecting NAS wipes saved SMB/FTP credentials, host endpoints, and active transfer queues from the app. Files already synced to your physical NAS hard disks remain safe and unaltered.
  • Google Play Subscriptions (Pro Tier): Critical Billing Rule: Uninstalling the app or wiping data does NOT cancel an active subscription billed through Google Play. You must manually cancel via: Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > LensDock Pro > Cancel.

3. Physical Photo Safety Guarantee

Your original photography files are completely safe:

  • Physical original RAW and JPEG images stored inside your camera's SD card are never modified, erased, or formatted by an app reset.
  • Images exported to your Android device's public DCIM or Pictures gallery are preserved outside the application sandbox.

4. Irreversibility & Contact

Because LensDock does not maintain central cloud catalog mirrors, local database wipes and preset clearances are irreversible. For questions or formal assistance, contact: support@lensdock.app

重要须知： LensDock 采用“本地优先（Local-First）”架构理念。在申请清除本地数据或解除服务关联前，请仔细阅读本协议。

一、 免登录访客模式与数据擦除

LensDock 核心功能无需集中式账号体系。若需彻底清除本应用在您手机上的所有数据记录，您可随时通过以下方式完成：

  • 进入 Android 系统设置：设置 > 应用管理 > LensDock > 存储和缓存 > 清除全部数据；或
  • 直接从手机上卸载 LensDock 应用程序。

二、 第三方服务关联注销与解绑

若您此前在应用内关联过第三方服务，请按照以下说明进行解绑与注销：

  • Google 账号与云端备份解绑： 解绑后本地存储的 OAuth 令牌和访问凭证将立即被物理抹除。您亦可随时在 Google 账号安全中心 撤销授权。解绑不影响您个人网盘上已保存的文件。
  • 私有 NAS 存储配置清除： 清除 NAS 配置将立刻抹除设备本地保存的主机 IP、SMB/FTP 认证密码及待办队列。已成功存入您私人 NAS 实体硬盘的照片不受任何影响。
  • Google Play Pro 订阅自动续费： 特别注意： 卸载应用或清除本地数据不会自动取消通过 Google Play 结算的周期性订阅。请前往 Google Play 商店 App > 头像 > 付款和订阅 > 订阅 > LensDock Pro > 取消订阅。

三、 物理相机原片安全保证

您的原始摄影资产受到绝对安全保护：

  • 保存在硬件数码相机内部 SD 存储卡上的原始照片，绝不会因应用注销或数据清除而被修改或删除。
  • 已导出至手机系统相册（DCIM/ 或 Pictures/）的原图和导出的 JPEG 文件完全保留在公共媒体库中。

四、 数据操作不可逆性与协助渠道

由于应用无中心化云端服务器托管您的选片库，本地数据库一旦清除将无法远程找回。若需技术协助，请联系：support@lensdock.app
