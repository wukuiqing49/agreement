账号注销协议与数据删除说明

生效及最后更新日期：2026 年 7 月 31 日

本说明帮助您区分退出登录、清除应用数据、撤销 Google 授权和取消 Google Play 订阅，并说明各操作会删除或保留哪些数据。

1. 当前账号形态

当前版本使用 Google 登录建立设备本地会话，但不在开发者服务器创建独立的 Batch Photo 云账号或个人资料数据库。应用在私有存储中保存 Google 账号标识、显示名称和邮箱，并使用账号标识的 SHA-256 值关联 Google Play 权益。

因此当前不存在可由开发者在服务器端删除的云账号。您可以按以下步骤删除本地账号资料和其他数据，并撤销 Google 授权。未来若增加云端账号，本说明与应用内注销入口将同步更新。

2. 操作前确认

当您执行退出登录、清除应用数据、撤销授权或提交删除请求时，表示您已阅读并理解相应操作的影响。本协议不会限制适用法律赋予您的权利。

  • 备份需要保留的图片、PDF、压缩包、模板导出或接收文件。
  • 退出登录、清除数据或卸载应用不会自动取消 Google Play 订阅，也不会自动退款。
  • 相册、Pictures、Downloads 或其他外部目录中的文件不会因退出登录而删除。
  • 清除后，本地任务、设置、缓存和应用私有接收文件可能无法恢复。

3. 删除账号相关数据的步骤

A. 退出登录

  1. 打开 Batch Photo，进入“我的”或“设置”。
  2. 点击“退出登录”并确认。
  3. 应用会清除本地登录状态、Google 账号标识、显示名称、邮箱和系统凭据选择状态。

不会删除：任务记录、设置、缓存、导出文件、应用私有接收文件、Google 账号、Google Play 购买记录或订阅。

B. 清除全部应用私有数据

  1. 打开 Android“设置” → “应用” → “Batch Photo”。
  2. 进入“存储与缓存”，选择“清除存储空间/清除数据”；也可卸载应用。
  3. 该操作会删除私有目录中的会话、任务记录、设置、权益缓存、临时缓存和接收文件。

相册、Pictures、Downloads、共享存储或自选目录中的文件仍需通过系统相册或文件管理器手动删除。

C. 撤销 Google 授权

  1. 访问 Google 账号第三方连接页面。
  2. 找到 Batch Photo 或对应连接。
  3. 选择移除连接或撤销访问权限。

撤销授权不会删除 Google 账号，也不会取消 Google Play 订阅。

D. 取消 Google Play 订阅

  1. 打开 Google Play 商店 → 头像 → “付款和订阅” → “订阅”。
  2. 选择 Batch Photo 并点击“取消订阅”。
  3. 权益通常持续至当前已付款周期结束，以 Google Play 显示为准。

也可访问 Google Play 订阅管理页面。购买记录、退款、税务及法定交易记录由 Google 按其政策保留。

E. 提交数据删除请求

若您认为发布版本启用的 Firebase Analytics、Google Mobile Ads、反馈渠道或其他开发者可控制系统中仍有相关数据，请向 wukuiqing@gmail.com 发送邮件，主题注明“Batch Photo 数据删除请求”。

仅提供验证和处理所需的最少信息，例如 Google 登录邮箱或账号标识的必要部分、希望删除的数据范围及所在国家/地区。不要发送密码、完整购买令牌、支付卡信息、原始图片或证件号码。我们会在适用法律规定的期限内处理；依法必须保留、用于安全/反欺诈/争议处理或不由我们控制的数据可能无法立即删除。

5. 注销后果

  • 重新使用账号功能时需要再次登录。
  • 清除应用数据后，本地任务历史、偏好和私有文件会丢失。
  • 重新登录后，有效购买可能通过“恢复购买”重新识别。
  • 已分享或传输至其他应用、设备或人员的副本不受本应用控制。

Account Deletion Agreement & Data Deletion Notice

Effective and last updated: July 31, 2026

This notice distinguishes signing out, clearing app data, revoking Google access, and cancelling a Google Play subscription, including what each action deletes or retains.

1. Current Account Model

The current version uses Google Sign-In for a local device session but creates no separate Batch Photo cloud account or profile database on a developer server. The app stores a Google account ID, display name, and email in private storage and uses a SHA-256 value of the account ID to associate Google Play entitlements.

There is therefore no developer-hosted cloud account to delete server-side. Use the steps below to remove local profile data and other data and revoke Google access. If cloud accounts are added later, this notice and the in-app flow will be updated.

2. Before Deletion

By signing out, clearing app data, revoking access, or submitting a deletion request, you confirm that you have read and understood the effects of the relevant action. This agreement does not limit rights granted by applicable law.

  • Back up images, PDFs, archives, template exports, or received files you need.
  • Sign-out, data clearing, or uninstalling does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription or issue a refund.
  • Files in Photos, Pictures, Downloads, or external folders are not removed by sign-out.
  • Local tasks, settings, caches, and private received files may be unrecoverable after clearing.

3. Steps to Delete Account-Related Data

A. Sign Out

  1. Open Batch Photo and go to Profile or Settings.
  2. Tap Sign out and confirm.
  3. The app clears local sign-in state, Google account ID, display name, email, and system credential-selection state.

Not deleted: task history, settings, cache, exports, private received files, your Google Account, Google Play purchase records, or subscriptions.

B. Clear All App-Private Data

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Batch Photo.
  2. Open Storage & cache and choose Clear storage/Clear data, or uninstall.
  3. This removes sessions, task history, settings, entitlement cache, temporary cache, and private received files.

Files in Photos, Pictures, Downloads, shared storage, or selected folders must still be deleted manually through your gallery or file manager.

C. Revoke Google Access

  1. Visit Google Account third-party connections.
  2. Find Batch Photo or the associated connection.
  3. Remove the connection or revoke access.

This does not delete your Google Account or cancel Google Play subscriptions.

D. Cancel a Google Play Subscription

  1. Open Google Play Store → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  2. Select Batch Photo and tap Cancel subscription.
  3. Access generally continues until the end of the paid period shown by Google Play.

You may also use Google Play subscription management. Google retains purchase, refund, tax, and legally required transaction records under its policies.

E. Submit a Data-Deletion Request

If you believe related data remains in Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, feedback channels, or another developer-controlled system enabled in a release, email wukuiqing@gmail.com with subject “Batch Photo Data Deletion Request.”

Provide only the minimum needed for verification, such as the necessary portion of your Google sign-in email/account identifier, requested deletion scope, and country or region. Do not send passwords, full purchase tokens, payment-card data, original images, or identity-document numbers. Requests are handled within applicable legal periods. Data legally required, needed for security/fraud prevention/disputes, or outside our control may not be immediately erasable.
