Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026  |  Application: LensDock
Local-First Core Principle: LensDock operates primarily as an offline desktop and tethered companion. Core functions—including camera connectivity, shutter release, parameter tuning, and photo review—require no account registration and perform no stealth cloud uploads.

1. Overview & Information We Process

LensDock is engineered for photographers who demand control over their creative workflow. Because we follow a local-first design:

  • No Mandatory Profile or Sign-in: You are not required to provide personal identity credentials (name, phone number, email) to control your camera or review photos.
  • Photos & Media Files: Photos transferred from your camera or chosen from your Android device stay strictly within your private device storage or user-selected folders. We never upload your raw files or photographs to centralized servers.
  • User-Configured Storage: Network transfers occur solely upon your explicit instruction (e.g., syncing originals to your personal local NAS or sending photos via local Wi-Fi).

3. Third-Party & Open Source Libraries

To provide high-fidelity offline decoding and metadata management, LensDock incorporates reputable open-source engines under compliant licensing terms:

  • ExifTool & Embedded Perl Engine: (Perl Artistic License / GNU GPL) Provides professional EXIF, IPTC, and XMP metadata parsing and sidecar (.xmp) file operations entirely within an isolated local Android sandbox.
  • LibRaw: (CDDL-1.0 / GNU LGPL-2.1) Extracts high-resolution embedded previews and thumbnails from Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon NEF, and Sony ARW files for rapid mobile culling.
  • NanoHTTPD: (Modified BSD License) Embedded lightweight local HTTP service used exclusively for point-to-point transfers and local network sharing inside user-authorized Wi-Fi networks. It does not open public Internet listening ports.
  • MMKV: (Apache License 2.0 by Tencent) High-performance key-value local storage for camera parameter presets and user UI preferences.
  • AndroidX Jetpack & Material Components: (Apache License 2.0 by Google) Foundational UI scaffolding, system insets management, and Lifecycle architecture.

4. Permissions & Purpose Justification

  • INTERNET: Required strictly for local network communication with wireless cameras (CCAPI, PTP-IP), personal NAS sync, and Google Play In-App Purchase license verification.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Verifies Wi-Fi state prior to initiating camera discovery to protect against unintended cellular data usage.
  • USB Host Permission: Prompted by Android when a camera is plugged in via OTG cable; access is scoped only to the connected camera device session.
  • Scoped Storage (SAF): LensDock uses Android's Storage Access Framework document picker. We never request broad MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions.

5. Data Retention, Rights & Contact

All local database records and project presets can be cleared instantly in the app settings or by uninstalling the application. Photos in your phone gallery and camera memory card will never be modified or deleted without explicit command.

For questions regarding this policy, contact: support@lensdock.app

三、 开源库与第三方技术组件披露

为实现高保真 RAW 选片与标准元数据写入，LensDock 严谨集成了以下开源引擎并严格遵从开源协议：

  • ExifTool 与嵌入式 Perl 运行时： (Perl Artistic License / GNU GPL) 在 Android 隔离沙盒中高保真读写 EXIF、IPTC 与 XMP 伴随文件（.xmp）。
  • LibRaw： (CDDL-1.0 / GNU LGPL-2.1) 高速提取 CR2/CR3、NEF、ARW 等全规格 RAW 文件的嵌入预览流，免去庞大云端解码开销。
  • NanoHTTPD： (Modified BSD License) 轻量级内嵌式 HTTP 引擎，仅用于用户授权的同一 Wi-Fi 局域网内点对点传片，不开放公网端口。
  • MMKV： (Apache License 2.0，腾讯开源) 用于高可靠存储调参预设、拍摄模板及用户界面日夜偏好。
  • AndroidX Jetpack & Material Components： (Apache License 2.0，Google) 基础架构组件与 Material 3 视觉系统。

四、 权限申请与使用目的披露

  • 网络访问权限 (INTERNET)： 仅用于局域网连接无线 CCAPI / PTP-IP 相机、与用户配置的私有 NAS 同步原片，以及 Google Play Pro 订阅状态验证。
  • 网络状态感知： 在扫描设备前核对 Wi-Fi 连接状态，避免意外消耗蜂窝移动数据。
  • USB 宿主权限： 仅在用户通过 OTG 数据线插入相机时由系统动态授权当前连接会话。
  • 存储访问框架 (SAF)： 通过原生文件选择器按需读写用户明确挑选的相册目录，不索取宽泛的 MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE 权限。

卸载应用或在设置中清除应用数据即可销毁所有本地配置与数据库。物理相机 SD 卡与手机相册内的原始照片均不受影响。
